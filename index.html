About

Hi 👋🏻

I'm Marcos, an AI/ML Engineer with 8+ years of experience turning ML/NLP research into production systems — from Search & Relevance to Generative AI and Agentic AI.

  • Small language models and fine-tuning
  • Representation learning and reranking
